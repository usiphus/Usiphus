# V17 Faculty Workflow Pilot Protocol

## Purpose

Determine whether V17 improves a real specialty curriculum workflow without changing clinical meaning or weakening faculty governance.

## Before the pilot

1. Select one specialty and one bounded curriculum segment.
2. Name the faculty owner and define who may approve mappings.
3. Use synthetic or institutionally approved teaching artifacts only; never place identifiable patient data in fixtures.
4. Record the current workflow and baseline authoring/review time.
5. Define access, retention, deletion, consent/notice and aggregate reporting rules.
6. Freeze the curriculum version and record its provenance.

## Tasks

- Validate representative cards, claims, sources, curriculum mappings, assessments and OSCE stations.
- Review suggested, pending, missing and conflicting mappings.
- Correct mappings and record correction counts.
- Change one competency in a controlled curriculum copy and inspect the impact report.
- Compare two release manifests and inspect changed fields.
- Re-run the same analysis and compare output hashes for reproducibility.

## Measures

Record task start/end timestamps with timezone, role (not personal name), mapping corrections, evidence traceability issues, reproducibility outcome and friction notes. Collect no learner-level data unless separately approved. Do not record personal identifiers in the template.

Report participant/task counts, missing data, median and range of elapsed times, mapping corrections and unresolved conflicts, evidence traceability issues, repeat-run reproducibility and qualitative friction themes.

Do not interpret a faster workflow as evidence of better clinical content or educational outcomes. A small pilot is usability evidence only.

## Exit criteria

Faculty can inspect and correct every suggested mapping; no suggestion is mistaken for approval; curriculum changes are traceable to affected artifacts; source files remain unchanged; outputs are reproducible; privacy controls are acceptable; and at least one repeatable workflow improvement is documented. If the pilot does not demonstrate improvement, defer interface/refactoring work and revise the workflow.
