# V20 Faculty Pilot Protocol

1. Select one narrow specialty workflow and use synthetic feedback and teaching artifacts.
2. Define the feedback purpose, audience, notice, retention period and escalation route before collection.
3. Keep formative reflection separate from summative grading and learner progression decisions.
4. Triage feedback into actions linked to exact artifact/objective versions; assign an accountable owner and due date.
5. Close actions only with completion evidence and a documented follow-up outcome.
6. Draft new artifact versions; review field-level diffs and comments against the exact version.
7. Require qualified faculty review of clinical accuracy, evidence, curriculum alignment, accessibility and media rights before approval.
8. Inspect generated JSON/CSV/HTML outputs for privacy leakage and verify the report does not echo raw feedback/comment text.
9. Establish role-based access, lawful basis/consent where applicable, secure storage, retention/deletion and incident response before any real feedback is processed.
10. Measure action closure and authoring friction in aggregate. Do not interpret confidence, satisfaction, completion or engagement as proof of learning or competence.

Exit criteria: documented feedback notice and retention; restricted access; traceable actions with follow-up; reviewable artifact diffs; faculty sign-off; and no unresolved privacy, rights or accessibility blockers.
